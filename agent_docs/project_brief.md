# Project Brief

## Product

- One-line vision: An original mobile prototype for scanning, filtering, and understanding fictional insider-activity signals
- Target users: Mobile users and ArkLab AI assignment reviewers
- Primary user outcome: Move from a market overview to a filtered fictional trade and understand its mock details within seconds

## Scope

- Must ship:
  - Exactly three connected screens: Market Pulse, Screener, and Trade Details
  - Typed local fictional data, correct search/filters, chart, disclaimer, accessibility, and submission artifacts
- Not in v1:
  - Live/real financial data, networking, backend, authentication, alerts, and portfolios
  - Copied layouts/content and in-product AI

## Principles

- Solve the user story before adding polish.
- Prefer boring, maintainable choices.
- Keep generated docs short and current.
- Verify user-visible work in the real product surface.

## Development assistance

- Codex may assist planning, implementation, review, verification, and troubleshooting.
- The candidate must review, test, understand, and accurately disclose all assistance.
